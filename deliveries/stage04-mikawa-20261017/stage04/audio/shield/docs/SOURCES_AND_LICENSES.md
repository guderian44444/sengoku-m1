This is a sound-design interpretation, not an authenticated recording of sword striking a shield. Attribution is retained voluntarily; processed sounds are not artist-approved versions.

## v6 modification
Reuses v5 processed wood-only composites; compresses the onset without changing its first-80-ms timing. Adds a pitch-preserving time-stretched body derived from the same original heavy wood clips, band-limited 110–1700 Hz, with a controlled decaying envelope. No new external sources or synthesized tones. Final 5 kHz lowpass and approximately −1.6 dBTP ceiling. WAV duration exactly 0.76 seconds.

## v7 latest edit (supersedes v6 duration)
V7 is a dry-tail revision of v6, not a new sound source: keep first 100 ms PCM unchanged, apply rapid exponential decay afterward, final 35 ms fade and truncate to 380 ms. No additional time stretching or new layers. The earlier v6 760 ms duration describes its predecessor only. Source and license unchanged.
