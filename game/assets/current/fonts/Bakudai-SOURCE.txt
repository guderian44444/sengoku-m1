This is the unmodified official Regular font. The Regular face retains the
original brush strokes; upstream warns that generated bold styles can merge
fine strokes. All game Control and canvas text shares this bundled font.
Noto Serif TC and SengokuReferenceFallback remain bundled only for glyphs the
brush face does not contain. No installed operating-system font is required.
